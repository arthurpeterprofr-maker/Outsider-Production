Signing in

The admin area is your site address followed by /admin, for example www.outsidersproduction.ma/admin.

RoleAdministratorReads contact form messages, edits all content, creates or removes editor accounts, changes their own password.
RoleEditorEdits the site content and their own password. Cannot see messages or manage accounts.
Forgotten passwordClick « Mot de passe oublié ? » (Forgot password?) under the form: a reset link is sent by email. After several failed attempts, sign-in is blocked for a few minutes for security.

03 · Publishing
03

The golden rule

Nothing is visible to the public until you click « Enregistrer » (Save). You can experiment safely.

Step 1EditChange the fields in the tab you need.
Step 2Aperçu(Preview) opens the site with your unpublished changes, updated live.
Step 3Enregistrer(Save) publishes immediately and creates a version in the history.

Gestures common to every list

GestureEffect
+ Ajouter (Add)Creates a new empty item at the bottom of the list.
Handle ⋮⋮ (drag)Drag and drop to change the order. The up/down arrows do the same, one step at a time.
BinDeletes the item. Before saving, the undo arrow brings it back; after saving, restore a version.
Image buttonUploads an image from your computer (6 MB max). You can also paste a link.
Undo arrowDiscards all unsaved changes.

04 · How to
04

Add
a new film

  1. Sign in, open Contenu du site then the Cinéma tab.
  2. Click + Ajouter (Add) at the top right of the list: an empty sheet appears at the bottom.
  3. Open the sheet by clicking its title, then fill in the fields (see the table on the next page).
  4. For the poster, click the image button next to the Affiche (Poster) field and choose the file.
  5. Move the film to the right position with the ⋮⋮ handle: the first in the list is the first displayed.
  6. Click Aperçu (Preview) to check, then Enregistrer (Save).

05 · How to
05

Update
press & festivals

Studio news (press quotes, festival selections) lives in the "Selections & press" section of the site.

Add a press quote

  1. Tab Textes FR, open the Sélections & presse block.
  2. Under Citations (Quotes), click + Ajouter, enter the Texte (quote) and the Source (media name).
  3. Do the same in Textes EN for the English version.
  4. You can also change the title, the intro sentence and the line under the laurels.
  5. Preview, then Save.

05 · Laurels

Add a festival selection

  1. Tab Lauriers (Laurels), click + Ajouter.
  2. Nom (Name): the festival (e.g. "Venice"). Détail: the section (e.g. "Orizzonti").
  3. Drag the laurel to the right place, then Save.
TipAlso add the selection to the film sheet (Festivals field, Cinéma tab) so it appears with the film.

07 · Resident artists
07

Resident
artists

  1. Tab Artistes, + Ajouter, open the sheet.
  2. Nom (Name), then Identifiant d'URL (URL id): lowercase, no spaces or accents (e.g. "sara-benali"). It becomes the address of their page.
  3. Catégories: one line per filter, using exactly the keys from the Filtres artistes tab (e.g. "directors", "morocco").
  4. Biographie in French and English.
  5. Film: a cover image and a video link (YouTube, Vimeo or .mp4), played full screen on the site.
  6. Photos: + Ajouter for each gallery image, drag to reorder.

10 · History
10

Version
history

Every Save creates a version. The last 10 are kept: if something goes wrong, go back in one click.

  1. Click the clock icon in the action bar.
  2. Find the version by its date, time and author.
  3. Click Restaurer (Restore) and confirm: that version is published immediately.

12 · How to
12

AI Studio videos
& The Voice Room

Each page has a full-screen opening video and a series of videos (projects for AI Studio, capsules for The Voice Room). On the site they loop silently; one click opens them full screen with sound.

Add or replace a video

  1. Upload the video to Vimeo or YouTube (public or "unlisted") and copy its link.
  2. Tab AI Studio — vidéos or Voice Room — vidéos.
  3. Vidéo d'ouverture (Opening video): paste the link in the Vidéo field; the Image shows while it loads.
  4. Projets / Capsules: open a sheet, fill in the FR / EN title, cover image and video link. + Ajouter for another video, ⋮⋮ handle to reorder.
  5. Preview, then Save.

12 · Partners and home page

Committed partners (The Voice Room)

  1. Tab Voice Room — vidéos, Partenaires (Partners) list, + Ajouter.
  2. Nom (Name) of the organisation or partner, Logo (transparent PNG, ideally white), optional Site web (Website).
  3. Without a logo, the name is shown in full. Replace or delete the sample "[Partenaire 0x]" entries.

The "Discover" banners on the home page

Two large banners lead to AI Studio and The Voice Room. They automatically reuse each page's opening image and video. Their texts are edited in Textes FR / EN, block Bandeaux « À découvrir ».

13 · Home page order
13

Home page
order

Choose the order of the home page sections and hide the ones you don't want to show for now.

  1. Tab Ordre de l'accueil (Home page order).
  2. Drag a row up or down (or use the ⌃ ⌄ arrows).
  3. Click the eye to hide a section: it is struck through and disappears from the site, without losing any content. Click again to show it.
  4. Preview, then Save.
The opening (title and scrolling banner) always stays at the top, and Contact cannot be hidden. If you hide a section linked from the menu (e.g. About), keep that in mind.

13 · Trailer and search

Add a film trailer

  1. Tab Cinéma, open the film sheet.
  2. Paste the YouTube, Vimeo or .mp4 link in Bande-annonce (Trailer, at the bottom of the sheet), then Save.
  3. On the site, the film sheet shows a "Trailer" block above the Netflix link; it plays in place. Without a link, the block does not appear.

Search and sorting on the Cinema page

Nothing to set up: visitors search a title or a director (accents and capitals ignored) and sort by Selection (your admin order), Newest or Oldest (Year field). Type filters are created from the start of the "Type" field: always write it the same way (e.g. « Long métrage · … »).

14 · Best practices
14

Best practices

QuestionAnswer
I can't see my changesDid you click « Enregistrer » (Save)? The button is greyed out when there is nothing to publish.
My image won't uploadIt is over 6 MB. Reduce it (JPG, 80 % quality) before uploading.
The English text didn't changeEach language is edited separately: Textes FR and Textes EN, or the Français / Anglais fields of a sheet.
I deleted a film by mistakeBefore saving: undo arrow. After: History → Restaurer (Restore).
A video won't playCheck that the YouTube or Vimeo link is public (or unlisted) and that embedding is allowed. Netflix links always open in a new tab.
Display orderIn every list: the admin order (⋮⋮ handle). For home page sections: « Ordre de l'accueil » tab.
RememberEdit → Preview → Save. Until you save, the public site does not change. And the history always keeps your last 10 versions.
